## Project Overview

Aurora AI is a comprehensive initiative that combines cutting-edge AI research, community building through events, and rapid prototyping of innovative AI solutions. As the founder and lead developer, I established this project to bridge the gap between academic AI research and practical applications.

## Research Focus Areas

### Machine Learning & Deep Learning
- **Neural Network Architectures**: Experimentation with various CNN, RNN, and Transformer models
- **Computer Vision**: Advanced image processing and object detection algorithms
- **Natural Language Processing**: Text analysis, sentiment analysis, and language generation

### AI Ethics & Responsible AI
- **Bias Detection**: Developing methods to identify and mitigate AI biases
- **Explainable AI**: Creating interpretable models for critical decision-making
- **Privacy-Preserving ML**: Federated learning and differential privacy techniques

## Event Organization

### AI Workshops & Seminars
- **Technical Training**: Hands-on workshops on Python, TensorFlow, and PyTorch
- **Industry Speakers**: Invited talks from AI researchers and industry professionals
- **Student Competitions**: AI hackathons and machine learning challenges

### Community Building
- **Regular Meetups**: Monthly gatherings for knowledge sharing and networking
- **Online Presence**: Active social media presence and technical blog
